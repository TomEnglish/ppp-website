The Science

Why Contrast Therapy Works

What people come back for.

❄

Reduce Inflammation

Cold exposure constricts blood vessels and reduces swelling, helping your body recover from workouts and injury.

⚡

Accelerate Recovery

Alternating hot and cold promotes blood flow and nutrient delivery to muscles, so you bounce back faster between hard efforts.

💡

Mental Clarity & Focus

Cold plunge triggers a norepinephrine surge — sharpening focus, elevating mood, and building mental resilience.

❤

Enhanced Circulation

The hot-cold cycle acts as a vascular workout, strengthening blood vessels and improving cardiovascular health.

🌿

Stress Reduction

Infrared heat relaxes the nervous system while cold exposure builds stress resilience through hormetic response.

💪

Feel Better, More Often

Regulars report fewer sluggish weeks and better sleep. The research on cold exposure and immune response is promising and still developing.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before booking.

General

What is contrast therapy?

Contrast therapy alternates between hot (sauna) and cold (plunge) exposure. This hot-cold cycling boosts circulation, reduces inflammation, speeds recovery, and delivers a powerful mood and energy boost.

Is the session really private?

Yes. When you book a time slot, the entire unit is exclusively yours. You won’t see another customer. Our smart lock system ensures only you can access the unit during your session.

Your code is yours. Everyone who uses the trailer needs to be booked, paid, and on a waiver, so please don’t pass it around.

What temperature is the cold plunge?

The cold plunge is set to 39°F — cold enough to get the full benefits of cold exposure.

What temperature does the sauna reach?

The Clearlight Sanctuary C infrared sauna reaches up to 170°F with full spectrum infrared heat.

How clean is the water?

The plunge runs continuous recirculation through a 35 square foot commercial cartridge filter with ozone disinfection, turning the full volume over roughly every 15 minutes. And you’re never sharing it mid-session, because the trailer is yours alone while you’re in it.

Is cold plunge therapy safe?

Cold plunge therapy is generally safe for healthy adults. However, we recommend consulting your physician before use, especially if you have heart conditions, are pregnant, or have other health concerns. All users sign a liability waiver and facility agreement before their first session. Eligibility requirements are confirmed at booking.

What's the minimum age?

18 and up to book and use the facility on your own. Anyone 16 or 17 may use it only with a parent or guardian who has signed their waiver and who stays with them for the entire session. Under 16, no.

Anything I can't bring?

No glass, no alcohol, no anything illegal, no pets, no open flames. And if you’re shooting content in there for anything commercial, just ask us first.

Private Sessions

Where are you located?

We’re parked full time in Landrum, SC. Same spot, seven days a week. You get the exact address and your door code when you book.

How do I book a private session?

Choose your session length, pick an available time slot, and pay online. You’ll receive a unique smart lock access code for your session. Just show up at your scheduled time — the sauna will already be preheated.

What's included in a session?

Every session is fully private — the entire unit is yours. Equipment is preheated and ready. Towels are on us for private sessions. You have smart lock access for your time slot with no staff interaction required.

Can I bring a friend?

Absolutely. Your session is private, so bring a friend, partner, or training buddy. Add each one to your booking at $15 a head. The sauna fits 4 and the plunge is one at a time, so you’ll rotate. Everyone needs to be on the booking and on a waiver before they walk in.

What's the First Session?

$25 for a full 45 minutes, one per person. It’s the whole thing, not a tour. If you like it, a membership is $80 and gets you 30 minutes a day, every day. It pays for itself on your third visit.

How long is a session?

Three lengths: 30-minute Quick Session ($30), 45-minute Contrast Session ($40), and the 90-minute Long One ($75). First-timers get 45 minutes for $25. Need a little more time? Add 15 minutes to any session for $10, up to twice.

Do you offer memberships?

Two, and both are on a Back to School rate right now. Solo Unlimited is $60 a month, normally $80. Duo Unlimited is $90 a month for two adults at the same address, normally $120. Both include one 30-minute contrast session per day, every day — a sauna round, a plunge, another round, out the door. Want longer? Add 15 minutes for $10 when you book, up to twice.

Sign up by September 30 and your rate holds through May 31. Month to month, cancel whenever.

What counts as a Duo?

Two adults living at the same address. Spouse, partner, roommate, whoever’s under your roof. You each get your own booking profile and sign your own waiver, so we always know who’s in the trailer.

Come together or come separately, doesn’t matter. Anyone who doesn’t live with you is a buddy at $15 a session.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancel more than 24 hours out and you get a full refund. Inside 24 hours it’s non-refundable, but we’ll reschedule you once if we’ve got the room. No-shows forfeit the session.

What if I'm running late?

Your door code works for your booked window only, and you need to be there inside the first 10 minutes. Later than that and the session’s forfeit, because the next booking is right behind you. Running behind? Text us and we’ll do what we can.

Event Rentals

What area do you serve for event rentals?

We deliver within a 50-mile radius of Landrum, SC, including Tryon, Campobello, Spartanburg, Greenville, and surrounding areas.

Is there a delivery fee?

Delivery is free within 20 miles of Landrum. Beyond that, fees are tiered by distance.

How much space do I need?

You’ll need a level surface that can accommodate our 8.5’ × 18’ trailer, plus room for safe access and setup.

Do I need a water hookup?

A standard garden hose connection is needed for the initial fill. We handle the rest.

What about electrical?

We’ll confirm power requirements during booking. We handle all the logistics.

How many people can use the equipment?

The sauna comfortably fits 4 people; the cold plunge is single-occupancy, one at a time. For larger events, guests rotate through.

How far in advance should I book?

We require a minimum of 3 days notice. For weekends, weddings, and events, we recommend booking 2–4 weeks ahead.

What's your deposit policy?

We require a 50% deposit to secure your date, with the balance due before your event.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancel with 7 days notice for a full refund. We also offer full refunds for weather-related cancellations — we always err on the side of the customer.

Do you stay on-site during the rental?

Standard rentals are self-service after we set up and orient you. An on-site attendant is available as a paid add-on, and is included for corporate events and weddings.

What about bad weather?

You can cancel for weather with a full refund. We’ll work with you to reschedule if possible.

What's included in an event rental?

Delivery, setup, the Clearlight Sanctuary C infrared sauna, Morozko MIB72 cold plunge, equipment orientation, and breakdown/pickup. See our packages for details.
